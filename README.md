# Webbie V1.0
**Your Personal Spider Companion**
---
This is a project developed by me (Nihal) for the Week 1 (PCB design) of HackClub's Halflife Event🔥
---
A custom motion controlled digital bot which is shaped like a spider (hence the name Webbie). It has been designed sit on your desk (can also be used as a locket) and showcase its personality on a display. It has been equipped with a microcontroller, buzzer, motion sensor, display and environmental sensor, all fitted in a compact spider pcb design.
